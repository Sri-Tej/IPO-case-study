# Zomato Limited — Initial Public Offering (IPO) Case Study

## Executive Summary
This case study delivers a comprehensive financial and operational evaluation of Zomato Limited's Initial Public Offering (IPO) in July 2021. It examines company background, offer structure, capital utilization, financial performance leading to listing, and post-market dynamics.

---

## 1. Company Overview & Industry Context
* **Business Model:** B2C food delivery platform, Quick Commerce (Blinkit), and B2B supplies (Hyperpure).
* **Market Position:** Major duopoly player in India's food delivery sector alongside Swiggy.
* **Key Operating Metrics:** Gross Order Value (GOV), Monthly Transacting Users (MTU), and Average Order Value (AOV).

---

## 2. IPO Key Details & Capital Structure
* **IPO Period:** July 14, 2021 – July 16, 2021
* **Issue Size:** ₹9,375 Crore (Fresh Issue: ₹9,000 Cr | Offer for Sale: ₹375 Cr)
* **Price Band:** ₹72 to ₹76 per equity share
* **Listing Date:** July 23, 2021 (BSE & NSE)
* **Listing Gain:** ~53% premium over the issue price on day one.

---

## 3. Financial Performance & Key Metrics
* **Pre-IPO Revenues:** Significant scale-up in order volumes with growing contribution margins per order.
* **Profitability:** Unit-level positive contribution margins achieved prior to listing, despite net operational burn from marketing and customer acquisition.
* **Use of Proceeds:** Organic growth initiatives, strategic acquisitions (e.g., Blinkit expansion), and general corporate purposes.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways & Strategic Risks
* **Growth Drivers:** Urban smartphone penetration, rising digital payment adoption, and expansion into quick commerce.
* **Risk Factors:** Intense market competition, regulatory changes regarding gig worker legislation, and profitability pressure.
